// a working philosophy, kept in public

Character is not declared. It is hammered.

Most philosophy is decoration — admired on a wall, never carried into a bad morning. This site holds the other kind: claims sharp enough to cut you if you're wrong about yourself.

A forge is not a museum. Nothing here is finished. The creed gets audited, the manifesto gets excerpted and argued with, and the failures get written down where they can be counted.

Everything you write on this site stays in your browser. No account, no server, no one reading over your shoulder.
