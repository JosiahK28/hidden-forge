// a working philosophy, kept in public

Character is not declared. It is hammered.

Most philosophy is decoration — a framed quote, agreed with and never applied. This is the other kind: a creed you're scored against, a record that remembers what you actually did, and claims sharp enough to cut you if you're wrong about yourself.

A forge is not a museum. Nothing here is finished. The creed gets audited, the manifesto gets excerpted and argued with, and the failures get written down where they can be counted.

Everything you write lands in this browser first, always — that's also the only way it can disappear. Sign in when losing it stops being acceptable, not before.
